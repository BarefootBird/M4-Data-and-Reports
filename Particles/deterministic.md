# Is the path of particles for bear spawns deterministic

A [full run](/Particles/data/fullRun.json) of f4 was recorded and all particles logged using the [particle logger script](/Particles/particleLogger.md)

![Full Run](/Particles/images/fullRun.png)

Bat and chicken particles were not filtered from this recording

The one line that dips down to y = ~70 is the bear particle line. As can be seen, all 4 bears spawns produced the exact same line, with every particle being in precisely the same location for each spawn.

# Conclusion

Bear particle trails are deterministic
